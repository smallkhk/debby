📡 Stream to OBS / Zoom / Meet

  1. Start the stream, then click OBS output — a clean, chrome-free window opens with just the AI feed.

  3. Click OBS Start Virtual Camera, then select OBS Virtual Camera as your webcam in Zoom, Meet, or Discord.
